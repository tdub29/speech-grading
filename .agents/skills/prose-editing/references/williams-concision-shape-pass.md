
# Williams & Bizup: Concision and Shape Pass

## When to use

Use when a passage is too long, hard to parse, clause-heavy, or padded but still needs to preserve complex thought.

Don't trigger for broad prose improvement when another skill owns the artifact. This skill should end in the concrete output named below.

## The framework

- Concision removes words that add no meaning: repeated ideas, obvious implications, unnecessary metadiscourse, and phrase bloat.
- Shape controls how complexity unfolds. Long sentences can work when their structure is legible.
- Grace is not just shortness; it is the fit between meaning, length, and reader processing.

## How to apply

1. Cut meaningless openings, throat-clearers, and redundant modifiers.
2. Replace phrases with words: 'due to the fact that' to 'because'; 'in the event that' to 'if.'
3. Prefer affirmative direct forms unless negation is the point.
4. Find sentence sprawl: stacked subordinate clauses, interrupted subject-verb connections, long abstract subjects.
5. Reshape with coordination, resumptive modifiers, summative modifiers, or sentence splits where each relation becomes clearer.
6. Verify no necessary qualification, evidence, or ethical nuance was cut.

## Common failure modes

- Cutting until the sentence is shorter but less accurate.
- Removing metadiscourse that genuinely guides readers through a complex argument.
- Turning every long sentence into fragments and losing relation among ideas.
- Keeping a long abstract subject that delays the verb beyond reader patience.
- Using negative phrasing where an affirmative sentence would be simpler.
- Mistaking concision for bluntness; tone can remain civil and precise.

## Output artifact

Produce: concision ledger, phrase-to-word replacements, sprawl diagnosis, reshaped rewrite, and accuracy-preservation notes.

Use this compact structure:

```markdown
## Concision and Shape Pass

### Diagnosis
[What is failing and why]

### Revision Plan
[Concrete edits/checks in priority order]

### Rewrite
[Before/after or revised passage]

### Watchouts
[Failure modes most relevant here]
```
